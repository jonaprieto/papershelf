What it does

One home for every paper

Browse the folders you already use, or add a single PDF. They stay exactly where they are.

Names you can trust

Every rename is proposed first, with what it was built from. Originals move to original_pdfs/.

Highlights that mean something

A palette you name yourself, so a colour carries a reason. Notes attach to the passage.

AI when it helps

Your endpoint, your key. The window says what it will send, and where, before it sends it.

Reading projects

Ask across the papers you chose

Every claim carries a numbered source, and every source shows the passage it came from — tap it and the page opens at it.
